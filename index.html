About me

I'm a UC San Diego Data Science graduate who likes the part of analytics where numbers meet people: figuring out what a team actually needs to know, then building the analysis that answers it.

I've analyzed payment data at a fintech, benchmarked AI platforms to shape sales strategy, and now work as a business analyst at The Bot Company, supporting manufacturing AI. I work iteratively. I'd rather put a rough version in front of stakeholders early and sharpen it with their feedback than polish something nobody asked for.
